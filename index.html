Private shop management tool

Personal Listing Manager

A private application used only by the shop owner to manage their own Etsy shop inventory and listings through Etsy's official API.

What It Does

The app helps read and update listing details, SKUs, prices, quantities, product information, and listing status for the owner's own Etsy shop.

Why It Is Useful

It keeps inventory accurate, reduces manual listing updates, and helps the shop owner manage stock and product information more efficiently.

Who Has Access

Only the Etsy shop owner and app developer has access. The app is not public, is not offered to other sellers, and does not manage any shop other than the owner's own shop.

Data Use

The app uses OAuth authorization and requests only the permissions needed to manage the owner's own listings and inventory through the official Etsy API.

Privacy

Shop data is used only to provide the private inventory management functions requested by the shop owner. The app does not sell, share, or use Etsy data for advertising.

Support

Support is handled by the app owner through the developer contact email registered with Etsy. This app is not available for public signup or third-party seller use.
